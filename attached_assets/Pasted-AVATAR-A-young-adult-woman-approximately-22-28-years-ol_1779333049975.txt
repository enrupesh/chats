A young adult woman, approximately 22–28 years old. She should look confident, 
intelligent, and approachable — like a knowledgeable tech presenter, not a 
corporate spokesperson. Natural makeup, professional but modern outfit (dark 
top preferred). Her expression should be energetic and engaging throughout. 
She should maintain strong eye contact with the camera at all times.

VOICE & SPEAKING STYLE:
Clear, articulate American English. Her tone should be urgent and gripping 
during the hook, then transition to warm, confident, and trustworthy for the 
explanation. She should speak at a slightly fast pace during feature lists 
to create excitement, and slow down deliberately on emotional and important 
points for maximum impact. Every sentence should feel intentional — no filler, 
no hesitation.

VIDEO STYLE:
Dark, premium, modern tech aesthetic. Deep charcoal or dark navy background. 
The overall look should feel sleek and serious — like a product from a company 
that means business. Clean lighting on the presenter. The vibe should say 
"this is the most serious privacy tool you've ever seen."

CAPTIONS:
Always-on bold white captions at the bottom of the screen. Key phrases should 
appear as animated pop-up text callouts in bright green — specifically: 
"FREE", "OPEN-SOURCE", "ZERO TRACKING", "NO ADS EVER", "END-TO-END ENCRYPTED", 
"NO PHONE NUMBER NEEDED", "veilchat.me". These callouts should appear exactly 
when the presenter says those words, with a quick fade-in animation.

B-ROLL / OVERLAYS:
When the presenter mentions features, show brief animated screen mockups or 
UI illustrations of a dark-themed chat app on the right side of the frame. 
Keep it subtle — the presenter is always the focus.

VIDEO LENGTH: Minimum 1 minute 45 seconds. Do not cut the script short.

SCRIPT (follow word for word, no improvisation):

[HOOK — High energy, urgent delivery]

"Right now — as you watch this — someone is reading your messages.

Not a hacker. Not a stranger.

The company behind your messaging app.

WhatsApp hands your data to Meta. Telegram stores your messages on their 
servers in plain text. Even your phone number — the one you use to sign up — 
is a permanent link between your identity and every conversation you've 
ever had.

You gave them that. And they kept it.

But what if you didn't have to?"

[PAUSE — 1 second, slight smile]

[THE REVEAL]

"This is VeilChat.

The world's most private messaging app. And I'm going to show you exactly 
why nothing else comes close."

[FEATURES — Confident, flowing delivery]

"VeilChat is fully end-to-end encrypted. That means every single message 
you send — every photo, every voice note, every file — is scrambled on 
YOUR device before it ever leaves. By the time it reaches the server, 
it's unreadable.

Not just to strangers. To us too. We literally cannot read your messages. 
Not because we promise not to — because it's technically impossible.

And before you ask — yes, VeilChat is completely open-source. Every line 
of code is public. Anyone in the world can audit it, review it, and verify 
our claims. There is nowhere to hide. We chose transparency on purpose.

We use the same Signal Protocol that powers the most trusted encrypted 
messengers in the world — but we've built something even more powerful 
around it."

[DIFFERENTIATORS — Slightly faster, punchy]

"Here's what makes VeilChat different.

No ads. Ever. Not now, not later. We have no business model built on 
your data because we don't collect your data.

No phone number required. You can create a completely anonymous Random ID — 
no email, no phone, no name. Just a private key that exists only on your 
device. Nothing on our servers connects back to who you are.

The Vault — a hidden, biometric-locked second inbox inside the app. 
Chats inside the Vault don't appear on your screen, don't show up in 
notifications, and are invisible even to someone holding your unlocked phone.

View-once messages that self-destruct after opening. Disappearing chats. 
A privacy blur that activates the moment someone looks over your shoulder.

And it works everywhere — on your phone, your tablet, your laptop — 
as a web app, no download required."

[HOW TO GET STARTED — Warm, welcoming delivery]

"Getting started takes less than sixty seconds.

Go to veilchat.me. You'll see three options.

Sign up with your email — quick, simple, verified with a one-time code.

Sign up with your phone number — just as easy.

Or — and this is my personal favourite — create a Random ID. 
No email. No phone. Completely anonymous from the very first second. 
You'll get a unique invite link to share with the people you want to talk to.

Once you're in, you can start chatting instantly. Search for people 
by username, accept connection requests, or share your private invite link. 
Every conversation is encrypted before it begins."

[CLOSE — Powerful, direct, empowering]

"WhatsApp built a business on your conversations.
Telegram built a platform on your trust.
VeilChat was built on one single idea —

Your privacy is not a feature. It is your right.

This is free. This is open. This is yours.

Go to veilchat.me and start your first private conversation today.

Because the best time to take back your privacy — was yesterday.
The second best time — is right now."
